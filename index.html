How does an atom really look?

For a long time, we pictured the electron orbiting the nucleus like a planet circling the sun.

But that model was rejected once we discovered that the quantum universe behaves in far stranger ways.

You are looking at a Quantum Probability Cloud. According to the Schrödinger Equation, electrons exist as a fuzzy mathematical pattern of probability density.

Every pearl represents a point where an electron is likely to be found when observed. The swirling flow is a stylized representation of the Probability Current: a circulation that orbitals with a non-zero magnetic number carry around their axis.

Parameters

n (Principal): Distance from the nucleus and structural energy tier shell. Bigger n means a bigger, higher-energy cloud, further from the nucleus.

l (Azimuthal): Geometrical shape of the spatial node lobes (sphere (s), dumbbell (p), cloverleaf (d), and other (f)). Must be less than N.

m (Magnetic): Which way the shape points, and the direction of the probability current. (-l to +l).
