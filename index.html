How does an atom really look?

For a long time, we pictured the electron orbiting the nucleus like a planet circling the sun.

But that model was rejected when we discovered that the quantum universe behaves in far stranger ways.

You are looking at a Quantum Probability Cloud. According to the Schrödinger Equation, electrons exist as a fuzzy mathematical pattern of probability density.

Every pearl represents an optimal point where an electron is likely to materialize when observed. The swirling flow represents the Probability Current: a physical momentum wave configuration driven directly by the magnetic quantum numbers.

Parameters

n (Principal): Distance from the nucleus and structural energy tier shell.

l (Azimuthal): Geometrical shape of the spatial node lobes (s, p, d, f). Maximum threshold matches n - 1.

m (Magnetic): Orientation alignment coordinates across an outer magnetic axis workspace fields (-l to +l).
